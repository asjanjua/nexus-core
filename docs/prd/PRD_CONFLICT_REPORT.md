# Pinavia PRD Conflict Report

Status: active arbitration record | Updated: 2026-08-02

## Decisions applied

1. **One core, not five platforms.** All product rooms reuse the NexusAI modular monolith, Clerk, Postgres/Drizzle, R2 evidence, model routing, audit, sensitivity, approvals, reviewer seat, and pilot lifecycle. A brand/subdomain is not evidence of independent runtime readiness (`docs/USER_STRATEGY_AND_PIVOTS.md`; `docs/API_SERVICE_BOUNDARY_DECISION.md`).
2. **NexusAI is the 30-day launch priority.** Pivot products are bounded pilots and only receive staffed deep work after a design partner, bounded workflow, reviewer, evidence, route/test proof, and SOW hypothesis are present.
3. **The public launch date is reset to 2026-09-01.** Historical July dates are retained only as prior-plan evidence. Pilot-signing is a 2026-09-15 target, OPEN for founder confirmation by 2026-08-05.
4. **No product may imply autonomous action.** Quorum does not approve minutes/send packs; Meridian does not file/certify; Vantage does not decide; Nucleus does not publish/client-send; NexusAI does not write back.
5. **The commercial rail is shared.** Free launch + Pro waitlist → paid SOW pilot → Stripe. Earlier pricing collateral is a commercial hypothesis unless governed by a signed SOW.

## Calendar capacity rule

Between now and public launch, shared engineering may undertake only: (a) the NexusAI public/pilot proof loop, (b) release controls and deploy evidence, and (c) one buyer-committed pivot’s signed-in pilot slice. All other pivot work is documentation, design-partner discovery, or explicitly post-launch. This avoids double-booking the same reviewer-seat, evidence, release, and commercial-paperwork capacities.
